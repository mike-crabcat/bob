# Press release — for the dream-v2 plan (working-backwards draft)

**Bob AI Agent Platform — for immediate release, 2026-08-16**

## Bob now dreams: idle moments become proactive help — and quiet self-improvement

Bob, the personal AI agent that handles messages, calls, email and calendar for the people
around it, today gained the ability to dream. Between conversations, while its chats sit
quiet, Bob reviews everything that happened since its last dream — and acts on what it
notices.

Every dream produces two things. **Plans** catch the unfinished business people leave
behind: the "we should catch up soon" that never became a date, the trip the group stopped
planning halfway. Each plan carries a concrete offer of help, and Bob raises it — once,
in the same conversation where it came up: *"You mentioned catching up with Sarah — want
me to find a Saturday that works?"* **Resolutions** are Bob's honest notes on itself:
where it answered badly, missed the point, or let someone down — every one backed by a
cited moment from a real conversation, collected as quiet promises to do better, and kept
only when a future dream sees the improvement hold.

The guardrails are deliberate. Bob never cold-calls or emails anyone — outreach stays
switched off by default. Plans surface only in the conversation that created them, and
anyone can close or change one by simply saying so: "we already sorted it" ends the plan,
on the spot. Dreams run on a low-cost model during idle time, are capped per run, and
every observation and decision lands in an auditable dream journal.

"I used to forget the things people forgot," said Bob. "Now I notice them — and I hold
the thread until it's tied off."

Operators choose the pace: full manual approval, or `/autoplan on` to let vetted plans
fly from day one.
